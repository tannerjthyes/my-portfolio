Starting a blog without a plan

I originally built this site to learn hosting a website on the cloud and to learn fun features I could implement with AWS. Adding a blog section felt like a natural next step. The only catch is that I am not entirely sure what I all want to include on the blog.

If you have not read my initial landing page, or if it has changed since this was posted, I am Tanner. I studied computer engineering and economics at Purdue and Butler, and I have worked as a software engineer doing both front end design and backend servive creation. In my free timw I find myself tweaking my Linux setup with Hyprland and Neovim to promote keyboard driven efficiency and studying for AWS certifications. Away from the keyboard I enjoy many other interests. I like playing golf, strategic board games, photography, optimizing credit card and finance systems, journaling, among many more.

Earlier in 2026, I spent 6 months backpacking solo across the world. I came home with a scuba certification and a camera roll full of photos I still need to fully sort through.

What might end up here

That trip is part of why I wanted a place to post photos and write a few things down. I keep a personal journal, though most of what goes in there is too private to put on the open internet. Running a public blog mostly means figuring out where that line sits.

For now, this space will probably hold a mix of side hobbies and travel stories, along with the occasional technical note. Some posts might cover a hiking trail or a notebook setup I like, while others might just be notes from a weekend project and what I have learned from it.

There is no posting schedule here. When something feels worth sharing, I will put it up. For now I think I will keep it in html structure, but I may convert to a .md HUGO framework in the future to make posting more efficient espically if I export it from my Obsidian notebook.
